// Tool 04 — A/B Test Tracker

Track what actually
converts.

Log A/B subject line tests, record real open or click rates, and see which pattern wins — with a running history of every test.

// How it works
01
Fill in the test Enter the email name, send date, and the two subject line variants (A and B).
02
Add the real rates Once the test is done, enter the actual open rate or click rate for each variant. Select the right metric from the dropdown (Open rate / Click rate).
03
Click Save & Compare The tool calculates the winner, shows the exact difference between variants, and logs the test to your session history below.
04
Track patterns over time After a few tests, the What's winning section shows your average winner rate and the subject type that converts most consistently with your audience.
→ Rates are optional — you can save a test while it's still running and it will appear in your history. Add the results later when the test is complete.
