Top Pattern Definitions
Guest Language CTA
CTA describes what the brand offers, not what the reader gains or does next.
Feature-First Bias
Email opens with the product's capability, not the reader's outcome or situation.
Filing Label Subject
Subject line names the content category instead of the consequence for the reader.
Buried Proof
Social proof, results, or trust signals appear below fold after the reader has left.
