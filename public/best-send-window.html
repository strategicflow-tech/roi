// What this tool is for
Optimal send timing for your specific email — not a generic benchmark
Most teams send at 10am Tuesday because someone read a blog post in 2019. This tool calculates the optimal window for your specific email type, industry, and audience.
Different email types have different optimal windows. A re-engagement email and a product update should never go out at the same time.
// Step 1
Select your email type
Each type has a different reader intent at different times. Onboarding emails work differently from re-engagement or promotional sends.
// Step 2
Select industry and geography
B2B SaaS and e-commerce have opposite optimal windows. Geography determines local time calibration — a 9am send means nothing if your audience is split across 4 time zones.
// Step 3
Read Why This Window
Understand the reasoning before scheduling. If your own data contradicts the recommendation, your data wins — use the optional field to add what you already know.
// Step 4
Check Days to Avoid
Higher-risk slots based on benchmark data from 51+ email audits and lifecycle performance patterns.
// Step 5
Use the Weekly Calendar
Share with your team as a send schedule reference. Green slots are safe for any send. Amber slots work for high-urgency sends only.
// Step 6
Split your list if over 10K
Staggered sends to engaged vs. less engaged segments consistently outperform single blasts on open rate and click rate.
