How ToolIndex works

Five steps — from free listing to full visibility.

01
Submit your product
Fill in your product name, URL, category, and description. Your listing goes live immediately with a permanent dofollow backlink from a DR 86 domain. No account required, no review queue, no cost.
02
Get discovered & collect upvotes
Any visitor can upvote your product once. Votes accumulate permanently and push your listing higher in the default sort. The Leaderboard tabs (Daily, Weekly, All-time, Biggest Climber) surface the most upvoted products each period.
03
Claim ownership
Find your product and click Claim. Verify with a one-time code sent to your email. As the verified owner you can edit your description (up to 500 characters), upload a logo, add screenshots, track real traffic, and receive vote notifications. Claimed listings show a ✓ Verified Owner badge.
04
Relaunch claimed owners only
Once claimed, you can Relaunch your listing once every 30 days — directly from the owner panel. Relaunching refreshes your timestamp and pushes your listing back to the top of the New Today tab, giving you a fresh wave of visibility at no cost.
05
Featured placement optional · from $9
Need more visibility fast? Click ⚡ Boost on your listing card. Daily Boost ($9) pins you at #1 for 24 hours. Weekly Feature ($19) gives you 14 days in the Featured Spotlight with a gold badge. Premium Listing ($29) runs for 30 days with a purple badge and top-3 placement.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use ToolIndex as a founder?

See the full step-by-step guide → for founders: submitting your product, collecting upvotes, claiming ownership, relaunching to stay visible in "New Today", and optional featured placements from $9.

How do I submit my SaaS tool to ToolIndex?

Click Submit your product at the top of the page, fill in your product name, URL, category, and a short description, then hit submit. Your listing goes live immediately and you receive a free dofollow backlink from our DR 86 domain — no account required.

Is it free to list on ToolIndex?

Yes, completely free. Every submission gets a permanent dofollow backlink from a DR 86 domain. Optional paid placements (Weekly Feature, Premium Listing, Daily Top) are available if you want extra visibility, but the base listing and backlink are free forever.

What is a dofollow backlink and why does it matter for SEO?

A dofollow backlink signals to Google and other search engines to follow the link and pass SEO authority to your page. A link from a DR 86 domain is high-quality and can contribute to your product's domain rating and organic visibility over time.

How do I claim my product listing?

Find your product in the directory and click Claim. Enter your email and we'll send a one-time verification code. After verifying, you can edit the description, upload your logo, and track upvotes. Claimed listings show a verified owner badge.

What categories of tools are listed here?

ToolIndex currently covers: AI Tools, Productivity, Marketing, Developer Tools, Social Media, Finance, Design, Analytics, Directories, and General SaaS. There are 500+ products listed across all categories, sourced from multiple aggregators and direct submissions.

How does upvoting and the leaderboard work?

Any visitor can upvote each product once — one upvote per listing per device, permanently (votes don't reset daily). No account needed. The leaderboard shows the top 10 products by votes in the current period. Products with more votes appear higher in the default sort order. Claimed owners receive weekly leaderboard rank updates by email.

How do I get my product featured at the top?

Click the ⚡ Boost button on any listing card. Options: Daily Boost ($9 — 24 hours as #1 in the grid, activates instantly, no review queue), Weekly Feature ($19 — 14 days in the Featured Spotlight with a gold badge), or Premium Listing ($29 — 30 days with a purple badge and top-3 placement).

What are all the paid options available for my listing?

There are 7 paid options across two categories:

Visibility boosts — Daily Boost ($9, 24 h at #1), Weekly Feature ($19, 14 days with gold badge), Premium Listing ($29, 30 days with purple badge + top-3 placement).

Ownership upgrades — Founder Pack ($49, 30-day Premium + unlimited Relaunches + priority brand carousel placement), Verified Badge ($9, permanent ✓ Verified badge on card and product page, no expiry), Teardown Spotlight Solo ($19, professional teardown of your landing page or email, published on ToolIndex within 48–72 h), Teardown Spotlight Pro ($49, everything in Solo + LinkedIn feature + Startup of the Week on the ToolIndex homepage).

Click ⚡ Boost from $9 on any listing card, or the ✨ Boost This Listing panel on any individual product page, to access all options.
