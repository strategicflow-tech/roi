FAQ

Why does content fail — and how does WHY.™ find it?

Content friction is any point in your email, landing page, or document where the reader's brain pauses, doubts, or disengages — before taking the action you want. It includes unanswered questions, vague CTAs, missing social proof, and structural logic gaps. Research on decision-making shows that unresolved friction is the primary reason high-quality offers get ignored. WHY.™ surfaces every friction point before you rewrite, so you fix the right things instead of guessing.
Low open rates are usually a subject line friction problem: the subject doesn't create enough consequence or curiosity to justify opening. Low click-through rates are a body friction problem: the email doesn't answer "what do I get?" clearly enough before the CTA. WHY.™ diagnoses both layers separately — it scores your friction, identifies which specific phrases cause hesitation, and generates a rebuilt version that leads with outcome rather than feature.

WHY.™ applies the Decision Friction Model, which maps the 15 questions a reader will ask before converting and checks whether your content answers them. Unlike general-purpose AI writing tools, WHY.™ is not trying to make your content "sound better" — it's finding the structural gaps that prevent a decision. The output is a friction score (1–10), five ranked friction points, and 15 predicted reader questions with blunt assessments of whether your content answers them.
A standard content audit checks for SEO coverage, word count, and readability scores. An AI writing assistant rewrites for tone or grammar. WHY.™ does neither. It works backwards from the reader's decision process: what questions would a real person ask before clicking, buying, or replying — and does your content answer them? This friction-first diagnostic approach surfaces problems that readability scores and keyword audits completely miss, particularly in B2B emails, SaaS onboarding sequences, and high-stakes proposals where one unanswered question loses the deal.
WHY.™ is built for SaaS founders, B2B marketers, consultants, and growth teams who write content that needs to produce a specific action — a trial signup, a reply, a purchase, a meeting booked. It works on emails, cold outreach, landing pages, pricing pages, proposals, investor updates, job postings, onboarding sequences, and ad copy.
