How to use Step-by-step walkthrough
▼
// What this tool is for
Emails that underperformed and are sitting in your archive unfixed
Every lifecycle team has 3–5 of these. This tool resurrects them in 90 seconds.
Best candidates: open rate below 15% on a non-cold list · click rate below 1.5% · onboarding emails where activation dropped · re-engagement emails that got no response · product update emails with low click-through
// Step 1
Paste the original
Subject line and full body text. The more complete the original, the more accurate the diagnosis.
// Step 2
Add performance data (optional but recommended)
Open rate and click rate tell the tool how severe the damage is. A 6% open rate is a different problem than a 14% open rate with 0.3% clicks.
// Step 3
Read Cause of Death first
Understand what killed the email before reading the rebuild. The rebuild will make more sense and you will be able to apply the same fixes to other emails independently.
// Step 4
Copy the rebuilt email
Click "Copy full email" and paste directly into your ESP. Subject line, preview text, and body are all included.
// Step 5
Check What Changed and Why
Use this section to brief your team or to document the fix in your email standards library.
