SaaS Launch Checklist: 23 Directories to Submit to Before You Go Live

August 3, 2026 · 10 min read · By ToolIndex

Most founders treat directory submissions as an afterthought — something to do after the launch. That's a mistake. Submitting before your first marketing push means search engines see your domain cited by high-authority sites from day one, giving every piece of content you publish a stronger starting position.

Direct Answer — AEO

Before launching a SaaS, submit to at least 10 free directories — starting with those with the highest domain rating (DR 70+). This ensures you have permanent dofollow backlinks in place before any paid traffic or SEO campaigns begin. ToolIndex (DR 86), G2 (DR 91), Product Hunt (DR 90), Capterra (DR 88), and AlternativeTo (DR 82) are the five highest-priority submissions for any new SaaS product.

Why Directory Submissions Should Happen Before Launch

When you launch a paid search campaign or publish your first blog post, Google evaluates your domain's existing authority. A domain with zero backlinks starts from scratch. A domain already cited by 10–15 high-DR directories enters the same competition from a much stronger position.

Directories take time to be crawled and indexed — typically 24–72 hours for most platforms. If you submit on launch day, you lose those first critical days. Submit a week or two before your planned public launch and you'll have indexed backlinks in place when your first visitors arrive.

Prepare your assets before starting: a 512×512 logo PNG, a 140-character tagline, a 500-character product description, your product URL, and your category (AI tools, productivity, marketing, etc.). Having these ready cuts total submission time from 5 hours to under 2.

How to Batch Submissions Efficiently

The single biggest time-saver is a submission document. Before opening your first directory, create a Google Doc or Notion page with these fields ready to copy-paste:

  • Product name — exact casing you want displayed
  • Website URL — use your canonical domain, not a subdomain
  • Tagline (140 chars) — benefit-first, not feature-first
  • Description (500 chars) — expand the tagline with use cases
  • Description (1000 chars) — for directories with longer fields
  • Logo URL — host a 512×512 PNG on your site
  • Category — pick the best fit from each directory's taxonomy
  • Founder email — separate from your support email

With this document open, each submission becomes a copy-paste exercise. Tier 1 submissions that normally take 20 minutes each drop to 5–7 minutes. The full 23-directory run goes from a full day to a focused half-day.
