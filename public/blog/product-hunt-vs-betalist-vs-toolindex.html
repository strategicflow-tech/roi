Founders default to Product Hunt because it's the best-known launch platform, but "well-known" and "fast" are different things. Here's how the three most common free listing options actually compare on approval time, backlink value, and long-term visibility.

Quick answer

Direct answer

Where each one actually helps

Product Hunt is built for a single launch-day event. If you get featured, the traffic spike is real, but it's a spike, not sustained discovery, and the odds of getting featured without an existing following are low.

BetaList is built for pre-launch validation, collecting early signups before you have a product. It's the wrong tool if you've already launched.

ToolIndex is built for ongoing discoverability: a permanent, searchable listing with a dofollow link, live the same day you submit, without needing to win a launch-day algorithm or wait out a multi-month free queue.

The practical takeaway

None of these are mutually exclusive. A common sequence: list on ToolIndex immediately for the instant backlink and permanent visibility, submit to BetaList if you're pre-launch and want early signups, and save Product Hunt for a coordinated launch push once you have an audience that can help you get featured.

FAQ

Is it worth submitting to all three?
Yes, they serve different purposes. ToolIndex for permanent dofollow visibility, BetaList for pre-launch signups, Product Hunt for a coordinated launch-day push.
What percentage of Product Hunt launches get featured?
Roughly 10% reach the front page, which is where most of the traffic and votes concentrate.
How long is BetaList's free queue?
Typically 2 to 4 months, unless you use the paid tier to skip ahead.
Does ToolIndex require a launch date or scheduling?
No, listings go live immediately on submission, with no scheduled launch day required.
