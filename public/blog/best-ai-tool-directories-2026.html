The AI directory clone problem

Search "AI tool directory" and dozens of near-identical sites appear, same card-grid layout, same category filters, same "trending" badge that resets weekly regardless of actual traffic. Most of these launched in the last year, running on templates sold as SaaS starter kits, with a DR under 30 and almost no organic traffic of their own. A backlink from a directory nobody visits does nothing for discoverability and very little for SEO, no matter how many of them you submit to.

What to check before submitting

Three checks take less time than the submission form itself: verify the DR on Ahrefs or a free equivalent, confirm whether the link is dofollow or nofollow, and check whether the directory's own listing pages actually rank in search or show up in AI-generated answers. A directory that doesn't get cited by AI search tools itself is unlikely to help your tool get cited either.

FAQ

How many AI tool directories should I actually submit to?
Four to six with verified DR above 60 cover most of the available authority. Submitting to dozens of low-DR clone directories adds time without meaningful SEO return.
Are nofollow AI directories worth submitting to?
They can still send referral traffic and visibility, but they do not pass domain authority the way a dofollow listing does. Prioritize dofollow first.
Why do so many AI tool directories look identical?
Many run on the same off-the-shelf directory template, launched quickly to capture search traffic for 'AI tools' without building real audience or authority.
Does a directory listing help a tool get cited by AI search engines like Perplexity?
It can, particularly if the directory itself is structured with clear comparison data and gets cited by AI search tools, which signals the underlying data is trusted enough to reuse.
