What Causes Delays Beyond the Normal Timeline?

Even fast-approval directories can get stuck if your submission has common problems:

  • Broken or redirecting URL: The most common rejection trigger — double-check your URL resolves to the correct page before submitting
  • Generic description: Descriptions that read like taglines ("an AI-powered platform for teams") often get flagged or deprioritized in editorial queues
  • Missing category: Submissions without a clear category mapping may sit in a "needs review" queue indefinitely
  • Product not yet public: Directories that require a live product (G2, Capterra, AlternativeTo) will reject applications for products under development — use BetaList for pre-launch instead
  • Duplicate listing: If your product already exists in a directory under a different name or URL, a new submission may be rejected as a duplicate
