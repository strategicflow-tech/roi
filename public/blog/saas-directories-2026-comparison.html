Which directories give instant approval

Most directories batch-review submissions, which means the "free" tier can sit in a queue for weeks. A small number, including ToolIndex, SaaSHub, and F6S, publish the listing immediately and review afterward. If your goal is a live backlink this week rather than this quarter, start there.

Why dofollow status matters more than the list length

A directory that nofollows every listing still sends referral traffic, but it does nothing for domain authority. Half of the "100+ directories" roundups floating around bundle nofollow aggregators in with real dofollow sources, which inflates the count without adding SEO value. Check dofollow status before you spend time on a submission form.

The honest time cost of doing this yourself

Manually submitting to 40 directories, filling out each form, verifying the listing, and tracking which ones are live, takes roughly 6–8 hours spread across a week, mostly because of approval queues rather than the submission itself. Batching submissions by approval speed, instant first, queued second, cuts that in about half.
