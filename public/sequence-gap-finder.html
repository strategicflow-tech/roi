What this tool is for

Low activation rates are usually blamed on copy. The real cause is usually a missing email — a transition that was never written because nobody mapped the journey end-to-end.

Sequence Gap Finder maps your sequence the way a new subscriber experiences it and finds the exact moment they lose momentum.

Step 1
Enter your sequence in order
Start with Email 1 — the first email after signup or trigger. Add every email in the order a new subscriber receives them. Include the subject line and one sentence on what you want the reader to do or feel.
The goal field is critical. Without it the tool cannot detect narrative gaps. "User clicks the CTA" is too thin. "User understands why the feature saves them 2 hours and books a demo" is the right level.
Step 2
Set the activation goal
What does success look like at the end of this sequence? Be specific:
  • "User creates first workflow" — not "user engages."
  • "User upgrades to paid" — not "user converts."
The more specific the goal, the more accurate the gap detection.
Step 3
Read the Activation Journey Map first
See your sequence the way your subscriber sees it. Red sections are where momentum breaks. These are your priority gaps — fix them before rewriting anything.
Step 4
Check gap severity
CRITICAL gaps are where activation stalls. Fix these before rewriting any existing email.
Adding one email in the right place will outperform rewriting three existing ones.
Step 5
Use the Missing Email Briefs
Each brief is ready to hand to a copywriter or use directly in Email Rebuild (Tool 02). Subject line variants are pre-written. You only need to add brand voice and specifics.
Step 6
Track the score delta
Run the tool again after adding the missing emails. The Sequence Health Score should move from below 6 to above 8 after critical gaps are filled.
