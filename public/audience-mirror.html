Audience
Mirror.

Paste real customer language. Extract the exact words that belong in your subject line, lead, and CTA.

How to use Step-by-step walkthrough
▼
// Step 1
Gather raw customer language
Best sources, in order of quality:
  • Replies to your emails — "I clicked because…"
  • G2 / Capterra / App Store reviews
  • LinkedIn comments on your posts
  • Support tickets describing a problem
  • Onboarding survey open-text answers
  • Sales call notes with direct quotes
Avoid: internal personas, survey data with closed questions, paraphrased summaries. Those are your words, not theirs.
Minimum 5 inputs. Sweet spot: 10–15. More inputs = more accurate language map.
// Step 2
Optional product context
One sentence about what your product does.
Example: "We help SaaS teams send product update emails that get clicked."

This lets the tool flag the gap between how you describe the product and how customers describe the problem.
// Step 3
Read the Exact Language Map first
These are the words that belong in your subject line and lead. Not your words — their words. Copy them directly into your next draft.
// Step 4
Use Before / After Vocabulary in the email
Before vocabulary → goes in the hook (name the problem they recognize)
After vocabulary → goes in the CTA (name the state they want to reach)
// Step 5
Check Words to Avoid
If your current subject line or lead contains any flagged words, rewrite before sending. These are the terms your team invented that customers never actually use.
// Customer language input
Customer language
Paste reviews, comments, email replies, testimonials, support tickets — one entry per line or separated by blank lines.
What does your product do? optional
One sentence. Helps the tool flag the gap between your marketing language and customer language.
