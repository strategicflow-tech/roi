How it works

1
Give us something to work with

Three ways to start:

  • Paste a URL — drop in any newsletter, blog post, product update, or changelog page. We fetch and analyze it automatically.
  • Paste the text — copy your email body directly into the text field.
  • Upload an HTML file — if you already have the email as .html, upload it directly.
2
Add context (optional but recommended)

Fill in your company name and original subject line if you have them. This helps us match your brand voice more accurately.

3
Hit Rebuild

We run your content through the Strategic Flow Method:

  • Outcome-first subject line that gets opened
  • Hook above the fold that creates immediate tension
  • Body copy that leads with consequence, not feature list
  • Single ownership-framed CTA that drives clicks
  • Brand colors, voice, and tone preserved throughout
4
Review the Before vs. After

You'll see your original alongside the rebuilt version, plus a breakdown of exactly what changed and why — subject line, hook, structure, CTA.

5
Download and send

Download the rebuilt newsletter as a ready-to-paste HTML file. Open it in your email platform (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Klaviyo, or any HTML-compatible sender) and send.

Frequently Asked Questions — Strategic Flow Email Audit

What does the Strategic Flow email audit analyze?

The preview evaluates five conversion signals: subject specificity, hook strength, feature-to-outcome translation, proof specificity, and CTA ownership. This is not a deliverability audit. We diagnose why your email fails to convert after it is opened.

What do I receive after the audit?

You first receive a 1–10 score and one actionable insight without entering an email. After unlocking, the full report includes named friction patterns, a rebuilt email, and downloadable HTML.

How long does the Strategic Flow email audit take?

The preview returns a score and one insight first. Paste your email body, then enter an email only if you want the full diagnosis and rebuild.

Is the email audit free?

Your first rebuild is free — no account or payment required. The rebuilt HTML and full diagnosis are included. Additional rebuilds are available via Decision Friction Review at $149 one-time or recurring plans from $299/month.

What is Feature-First Bias in SaaS emails?

Feature-First Bias is when a SaaS email leads with what the product does instead of what the reader gains. Example: "We've added custom reporting" instead of "You can now see which campaigns actually drove revenue." Found in 83% of audited emails. The fix: lead with the reader consequence, name the feature second.

What is a Filing Label Subject line?

A subject line that announces the topic like a folder tab — "New Feature: Reporting Dashboard" — instead of naming a reader consequence. It tells the reader what the email is about, not why opening it changes anything for them. Appears in 83% of audited emails. The fix: replace the label with a consequence, outcome, or tension.

What is Guest Language CTA?

"Learn more", "Explore what's new", "View update" — these describe the brand's offer from the brand's perspective. Guest Language treats the reader as a visitor rather than an owner. Found in 96% of audited emails. The fix: rewrite CTAs in first-person ownership language: "Fix my reporting", "See my data", "Get my first lesson."

What is the difference between a deliverability audit and an architecture audit?

Deliverability audits check whether your email reaches the inbox — SPF, DKIM, DMARC, sender reputation. Architecture audits check whether your email converts after it is opened. Most SaaS emails reach the inbox. They fail at conversion. These are two separate problems requiring two separate diagnostics.

What email types can I audit?

Any SaaS email: onboarding, product update, trial conversion, re-engagement, upsell, feature launch, newsletter, event announcement. Paste the text directly into the form or submit a URL to a live page. The system classifies the email type automatically and applies type-specific diagnosis.

How is the 1–10 email score calculated?

The primary audit evaluates five conversion signals on a 0–2 scale: subject specificity, hook strength, feature-to-outcome translation, proof specificity, and CTA ownership. The signals combine into the 1–10 score.

Is Strategic Flow's Decision Friction Model related to organizational decision-friction frameworks or other consulting firms named Strategic Flow?

No. Strategic Flow's Decision Friction Model is a five-signal diagnostic framework for SaaS email and content architecture, built by Alex Iliescu from 59+ real email teardowns. It is not related to organizational decision-friction models used in leadership or operations consulting, and it is not affiliated with another company or coach using the name Strategic Flow.
