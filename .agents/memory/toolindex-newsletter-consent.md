---
name: ToolIndex newsletter consent
description: Consent boundary between founder marketing and operational listing-owner email.
---

ToolIndex promotional founder emails require explicit consent followed by email confirmation. Never infer marketing consent from a claim, submission, extracted contact address, or verified ownership alone.

**Why:** Claiming a listing proves ownership but does not grant permission for recurring marketing. A central confirmed audience also makes deduplication, global unsubscribe, cooldown, and replay protection consistent across campaigns.

**How to apply:** Select recurring newsletters and promotional alerts only from confirmed, non-unsubscribed contacts. Transactional/operational owner notices may still be sent for their core purpose, but promotional sections must be omitted unless that owner is confirmed.
