WHY Audit, Strategic Flow Audit, and Blink Test must be excluded from every synthetic vote path: automatic boosts, daily/reseed flows, and admin seeding tools. Their existing counts are left intact and genuine visitor voting continues normally.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a vote-generation path, ensure it respects the protected listing set. Do not reset or delete existing votes unless the owner explicitly asks for a cleanup.
