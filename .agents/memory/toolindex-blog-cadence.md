---
name: ToolIndex blog cadence
description: Publishing schedule and ordering policy for the ToolIndex blog.
---

ToolIndex blog publishing runs on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays at 08:00 UTC. The blog list and “latest” card must always order articles newest-first by their publication date.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed this cadence and expects readers never to see a newer article below older entries.

**How to apply:** Preserve the cron cadence unless the owner changes it, and ensure any imported, generated, or manually added post is sorted by date before it is presented.
