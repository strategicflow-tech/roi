Add Tool 17 — Dead Email Resurrector to the 
Architecture dashboard.

WHAT IT DOES:
User pastes an underperforming email (subject line + 
body). The tool diagnoses exactly why it failed using 
the 6 Strategic Flow failure patterns, then delivers 
a complete rebuilt version ready to send — not a 
report, a new email.

INPUT:
- Field 1: Original subject line
- Field 2: Original email body (full text)
- Field 3 (optional): "What was the open rate?" 
  (helps calibrate severity)
- Field 4 (optional): "What was the click rate?"
- Field 5 (optional): "What was the goal of this email?"
  (activate / convert / re-engage / inform)
- Button: "Resurrect this email →"

OUTPUT — 4 sections:

1. CAUSE OF DEATH
Which failure patterns killed this email.
For each pattern found:
- Pattern name (exact label)
- Severity: CRITICAL / MAJOR / MINOR
- The exact line from the original email that 
  triggered the pattern
- One sentence explaining why it killed performance

Format:
❌ CRITICAL — Filing Label Subject
"Your April Product Update" — announces the topic, 
names no consequence. Reader has no reason to open.

2. RESURRECTION SCORE
Original score /10
Projected rebuilt score /10
Estimated open rate lift: +X%
Estimated click rate lift: +X%

3. THE REBUILT EMAIL
Full rebuilt version — ready to copy and paste 
into any ESP.

Includes:
- New subject line (curiosity gap or consequence-first)
- New preview text (extends subject, never repeats it)
- Rebuilt lead (consequence before context)
- Rebuilt body (feature-to-outcome translation)
- Rebuilt CTA (ownership language)

Format the rebuilt email exactly as it would appear 
in an inbox — subject line labeled, preview text 
labeled, then full body.

Add "Copy full email" button that copies everything 
to clipboard in one click.

4. WHAT CHANGED AND WHY
3-5 bullet points explaining the structural changes.
Each point: what was wrong → what was fixed → 
why it improves performance.
Specific to this email — never generic.

IN-TOOL WALKTHROUGH:
Collapsible "How to use" section at top of page.

Content:

WHAT THIS TOOL IS FOR:
Emails that were sent, underperformed, and are 
sitting in your archive unfixed. Every lifecycle 
team has 3-5 of these. This tool resurrects them 
in 90 seconds.

BEST CANDIDATES FOR RESURRECTION:
- Open rate below 15% on a non-cold list
- Click rate below 1.5%
- Onboarding emails where activation dropped off
- Re-engagement emails that got no response
- Product update emails with low click-through

STEP 1 — PASTE THE ORIGINAL
Subject line + full body text.
The more complete the original, the more accurate 
the diagnosis.

STEP 2 — ADD PERFORMANCE DATA (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)
Open rate and click rate tell the tool how severe 
the damage is. A 6% open rate is a different 
problem than a 14% open rate with 0.3% clicks.

STEP 3 — READ CAUSE OF DEATH FIRST
Understand what killed the email before reading 
the rebuild. The rebuild will make more sense and 
you will be able to apply the same fixes to other 
emails independently.

STEP 4 — COPY THE REBUILT EMAIL
Click "Copy full email" and paste directly into 
your ESP. Subject line, preview text, and body 
are all included.

STEP 5 — CHECK WHAT CHANGED AND WHY
Use this section to brief your team or to document 
the fix in your email standards library.
