SECTION TITLE: Tool 15 — Email Fatigue Detector

WHAT IT DOES:
Most lifecycle sequences are built by multiple people 
over months. Nobody reads them end-to-end. Nobody knows 
where the reader drops off — until the unsubscribe spike 
happens. This tool reads your entire sequence as one unit 
and finds the structural problems invisible in per-email 
metrics.

Four things it catches that your ESP never will:

1. CTA Fatigue — when the same action verb appears in 
3+ consecutive emails, click intent drops by ~40%. 
The tool maps every CTA across the sequence and flags 
the exact repetition pattern.

2. Narrative Drift — when email 5 talks about something 
email 1 never set up, the reader feels lost without 
knowing why. The tool identifies the email where the 
narrative breaks from the original promise.

3. Hook Recycling — opening with a similar structure in 
multiple emails trains readers to skip the first lines. 
The tool detects recycled hook patterns across the sequence.

4. Cadence Risk — email density + content overlap = 
predictable fatigue. The tool rates your sequence 
LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL and tells you exactly 
which gap to add or which email to cut.

HOW TO USE:

Step 1 — Paste your emails in sequence order.
Each block needs: subject line + body text.
Minimum 3 emails. Maximum 12.
Tip: paste them in the order a new subscriber receives them.

Step 2 — Click "Analyze Sequence →"
Results generate in 60-90 seconds.

Step 3 — Read the Sequence Health Score first.
Below 6/10 means structural work needed before 
optimizing individual emails.

Step 4 — Fix in priority order:
Start with CTA Fatigue. Then Narrative Drift. 
Then Hook Recycling and Cadence Risk.

WHO THIS IS FOR:
Any team sending a lifecycle sequence of 3+ emails:
onboarding flows, trial nurture, post-purchase, 
re-engagement, product update sequences.

Especially useful if:
- The sequence was built by more than one person
- It has not been audited end-to-end in over 6 months
- Open rates are fine but activation is below benchmark
- You are about to redesign the sequence and want to 
  know what is broken before rewriting everything

Route: /fatigue-detector
